MConnect

Listen to your DAW mix on your phone. Put MConnect on your master or listen bus, scan the QR code and tap Listen in the phone browser.

AU + VST3 · Apple Silicon + Intel

Requires macOS 11 or later. Both plugin formats are installed for your current user account. An existing version is replaced in the same plugin folders.

Close your DAW before installing. After installation, reopen it and rescan plugins if needed. No Mac restart is required.

Keep your phone and computer on the same local network. Use Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.
